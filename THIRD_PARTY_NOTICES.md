# Third-party map attribution

The South Korea map used by WithBiz is from `@svg-maps/south-korea` 2.0.0, by Dorian, distributed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Source: https://github.com/VictorCazanave/svg-maps/tree/master/packages/south-korea

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

WithBiz adds interactive counts, colors, selection and Korean labels. The app also displays a map attribution link.
